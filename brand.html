Calm. Precise. Recognizable.

Petrol for the seal and both tools, copper for the impression, neutral charcoal for dark surfaces. The same tokens drive the application and these pages.

  • Use the matching light/dark logo. Use black or white when you have one ink.
  • Keep one quarter of the symbol’s height clear on every side. The horizontal lockup works down to 180 px; below that use the symbol or the head.
  • Don’t stretch the mark, retype the wordmark, recolour the key or press, or add effects. The copper belongs to the impression only.
