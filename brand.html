Seal + Forge

The story behind the name

SealForge brings the idea of a seal together with the care of a workshop.

Seal — identity and integrity

In English, seal means both the animal and a mark of identity and integrity. One seal head brings the two together in a compact stamp.

Forge — craft

A forge is a workshop where skill turns an input into a finished result. SealForge brings that care to authorised signing: a verifiable signature, with the private key kept in its owner's token or HSM.
