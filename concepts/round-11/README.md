# Round 11 — One compact emblem

A new, additional logo exploration. Round 9 and Round 10 remain unchanged.
The current application identity and corporate downloads are not replaced.

## Scope and directions

Only one compact seal-head/short-neck symbol per candidate: no separate full seal,
no second large-format animal, no tail, arms, props or signing scene.

- **A / Cove:** quiet right-profile filled silhouette. Few large negative cuts;
  the simplest direction, with less facial nuance.
- **B / Pip:** warm expressive three-quarter mascot. Most playful; its eyes and
  muzzle contain more information and need care at very small sizes.
- **C / Harbor:** rounded balanced character. Preferred starting point for a
  friendly product emblem with fewer detached details than the fuller mascot.

The two owner-supplied stock images informed broad shape economy and character
warmth only. They are not redistributed as deliverables, traced, or cleaned of
watermarks. The concepts are newly generated studies; no legal originality or
trademark-clearance guarantee is implied. Reference URLs are in `study.json`.

## Outputs and limits

The actual canonical palette is retained. A normalized square alpha mask drives
all light/dark variants. Single-ink interiors are flattened instead of preserving
paper texture. 32/64 pixel previews are generated from that same alpha mask and
have no visible edge clipping; they are direct reductions, not optically tuned
final icons. White negative space is transparent so both backgrounds work.

The page compares all three marks, provides full-size boards, and uses the selected
single mark in a compact header and an app tile. These are identity-placement
sketches, not screenshots of a deployed application. The app tile intentionally
uses mint on neutral charcoal in both page tones. Study controls never write the
production theme preference.

The artwork is raster, not final vector source. No corporate kit is regenerated.
`study.json` records source hashes and delivered file hashes. Browser contract:
`tests/concepts-round11.test.cjs` via the existing loopback `tools/test-local.py`.
