# Round 5 — hold the key, keep the seal

Exploration only. The earlier workshop package is not the selected final identity.
No kit is generated in this round and no production application or brand asset is replaced.

## A / Held key

- Keep the natural profile and the small maker's-stamp action from Round 4.
- Make the grip explicit: the flipper contacts the ring and the key hangs outside
  the body, not as a badge on the chest.
- Strong at explaining the workshop action; weaker at reducing the number of props.

## B / Direct imprint

- Keep the natural profile and visibly held key.
- Remove the separate stamp tool: the working flipper makes the circular imprint
  directly on the document.
- Strong at a quieter, simpler scene; the forge/workshop story is less literal.

## Shared choices

- Coast & Copper; neutral charcoal `#151b1d` for dark backgrounds.
- The small icon is the actual head from its corresponding main drawing. The
  eye, muzzle and original face pixels are retained; only the neck is closed.
- There is no independently invented head/tool/document pictogram.
- 64/32/16 px images are studies. Tiny-pixel optical weight, final vector geometry
  and trademark clearance are not claimed.
- English page copy; no build dependency, framework or production theme storage.

Open `index.html` for the pair, `grip.html` or `imprint.html` for an individual study,
and `concepts.png` for a single comparison board. The theme button changes the
